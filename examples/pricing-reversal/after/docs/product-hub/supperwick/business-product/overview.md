# Supperwick – overview

Supperwick turns a household's dinner plan for the week into one shopping list, sorted by shop aisle.

## Main flow

1. Pick dinners for the week from saved recipes.
2. Supperwick merges the ingredients and subtracts what is already in the pantry.
3. Export the shopping list to the phone.
