# Supperwick – value proposition

## Customer and problem

Households of two to five people who cook most evenings and write the shopping list by hand, recipe by recipe.

## Alternatives

A paper list, a notes app or a recipe site's per-recipe list. None of them merges a whole week or knows what is already at home.
