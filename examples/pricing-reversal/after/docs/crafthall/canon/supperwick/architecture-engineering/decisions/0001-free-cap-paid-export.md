# ADR-0001 – Free plan capped, shopping-list export paid

**Status:** accepted 2026-03-02. **Superseded by [ADR-0002](0002-every-feature-free.md) on 2026-04-14.**

## Decision

The free plan covers up to three weekly plans a month. The shopping-list export is in the paid Plus plan; for a free user the export button opens an upgrade prompt.

## Consequences

The export is the reason to upgrade, so the free plan never shows a full week's list.
