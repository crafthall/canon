# ADR-0002 – Every current feature is free

**Status:** accepted 2026-04-14. Supersedes [ADR-0001](0001-free-cap-paid-export.md).

## Context

In the beta, most households stopped at the upgrade prompt before they had seen one full week planned, so they never reached the moment the product is for.

## Decision

Weekly plans and the shopping-list export are free for everyone, with no monthly cap. A later paid Plus plan adds new capabilities only and never moves a free one behind the upgrade prompt.

## Consequences

The upgrade prompt is removed from R1. Every page that states the free and paid split follows this record.
