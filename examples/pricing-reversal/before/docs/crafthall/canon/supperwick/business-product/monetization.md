# Supperwick – monetization

## Free and paid plans

Every current feature is free, with no monthly cap: weekly plans and the shopping-list export ([ADR-0002](../architecture-engineering/decisions/0002-every-feature-free.md)). A paid Plus plan will only add new capabilities; it never takes a free one away.

## Revenue gate

Plus is designed only after 200 households plan a full week (the R1 exit gate in the [roadmap](roadmap.md)).
