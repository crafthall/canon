# Shopping-list export

## What it does

Merges the week's ingredients into one list sorted by shop aisle and sends it to the phone. Free for everyone since [ADR-0002](../../architecture-engineering/decisions/0002-every-feature-free.md); until then it was in the paid Plus plan ([ADR-0001](../../architecture-engineering/decisions/0001-free-cap-paid-export.md)).
