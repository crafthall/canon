# <Name> – competitive index

How <Name> compares with the alternatives a customer would consider, scored on the jobs that matter, and how much each alternative overlaps with it. Optional page; it backs the "Alternatives" section of the [value proposition](value-proposition.md).

## Method

_What goes here:_ the dimensions (jobs) compared, a 0-5 scale with anchors, weights summing to 100 %, and the two indices: **competitive index** = weighted score (0-5) per product; **overlap index** = share of <Name>'s weight that an alternative also covers at 3 or more.

## Score table

_What goes here:_ rows = dimensions with weights, columns = <Name> and each alternative; every score with a one-line justification and a source (URL with access date, or repo path). Score <Name>'s real state today, gaps included.

## Overlap verdict

_What goes here:_ one row per alternative – overlap none / partial / substantial, the shared job, complement or substitute.

## Where <Name> wins and loses

_What goes here:_ the strongest and weakest dimensions, and the positioning line that follows from the index.

## Implications

_What goes here:_ which gaps to close first to raise the index, and what the overlap means for screening (knock-out K2) and pricing.
