## Customer

_What goes here:_ the person who finds and uses <Name> – market, language, situation, how they search for it (pull channel). One paragraph; link the research.

## Problem

_What goes here:_ the job they try to get done and what goes wrong today, in their words where research has them.

## Value

_What goes here:_ what they get from <Name> that they did not have – the outcome, not the feature list.

## Alternatives
