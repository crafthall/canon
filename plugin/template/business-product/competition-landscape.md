# <Name> – competition landscape

Appendix of the business plan (`business-plan.md`): who else serves <Name>'s customers and where <Name> stands among them. Optional page. The scored comparison the hub view shows lives in the [competitive index](competitive-index.md) when there is one – this page describes the market, it does not score it again.

## Market map

_What goes here:_ the segments of the market and the kinds of players in each (direct competitors, substitutes, do-it-yourself), with the size or growth figures the plan relies on and their source (URL with access date).

## Competitor profiles

_What goes here:_ one row per competitor – what they offer, who they sell to, their price, their strength and weakness, and a source with its access date.

| Competitor | Offer | Customer | Price | Strength | Weakness | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Position of <Name>

_What goes here:_ where <Name> sits on the map, the gap it fills and why a customer would switch; each claim marked as evidence (link) or hypothesis.

## Threats and openings

_What goes here:_ moves that could close the gap (a competitor's roadmap, a platform change, a price war) and the openings the plan counts on, each with what would show it happening.
