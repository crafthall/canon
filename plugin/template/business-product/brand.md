# <Name> – brand identity

The one page that says how <Name> looks and sounds in public. Design source files (logos, tokens, illustrations, proofs of concept) stay in `docs/design/<brand>/`; this page links them and says which one is current. Mission and value proposition: [overview](overview.md) (link, do not copy).

## Name

_What goes here:_ the public name, the umbrella brand if any, the internal code name, and the public identifiers (domain, package, plugin, repository) with the owner decision that set them.

## Idea and voice

_What goes here:_ the brand idea in two or three sentences, how it supports the mission, and the rules that keep it honest (what the copy never claims, what it never looks like).

## Logo

_What goes here:_ the chosen mark with its files, the variants (primary, reversed, one-colour, favicon), minimum sizes, clear space and what not to do; the concepts not chosen in one line.

## Colour and type

_What goes here:_ the palette as tokens with their use, the contrast check (WCAG AA), the fonts and how they are self-hosted.

## Where the brand is used

_What goes here:_ a table of every surface (website, app, plugin listing, OG image, README, social) with its state and the issue or PR that brought it there.

## Open decisions

_What goes here:_ what the owner still has to decide (headline, clearance), each with its issue.
