# ADR-NNNN – <decision title>

Copy this file to `NNNN-<slug>.md` with the next free number, set `adr` to that number and add its row to the [decision index](README.md). When this record replaces an earlier one, add `supersedes: <number>` here and `superseded-by: <this number>` in the earlier record, and set that record's status to `superseded`. The format follows MADR.

## Context

_What goes here:_ the problem and the forces behind it – constraints, requirements, what happens if nothing is decided – with links to the issue or the page that raised it.

## Options considered

_What goes here:_ each option with its pros and cons, the chosen one included.

## Decision

_What goes here:_ the option chosen and the main reason, in one or two sentences, plus who decided it.

## Consequences

_What goes here:_ what becomes easier and what becomes harder, the follow-up work, and the pages this decision changes.

## Confirmation

_What goes here:_ how anyone can check the decision is followed – a test, a lint rule, a check in review.
